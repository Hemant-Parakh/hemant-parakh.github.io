Research

Master's thesis, with Hapag-Lloyd AG

▾

Jun 2026 to Dec 2026 · supervised by Prof. Dr. Martin Kliesch, TU Hamburg

Data-Driven Decision Support for Forecasting and Driver Analysis in Maritime Trade. The question is whether outside information (macroeconomic indicators, peer companies' share prices, sentiment from maritime news headlines) really improves a carrier's own demand forecast four weeks out, and which drivers stay useful over time instead of looking good in one lucky period.

The models range from "same as last week" baselines to gradient boosting, all tested with walk-forward validation, with SHAP values for the driver analysis. Every experiment was written down before any of them ran. The data starts in 2020, so it has been through a pandemic, a boom, a bust and the Red Sea crisis.

walk-forward validationSHAPgradient boostingnews sentiment

Consumer Perception of AI-Based Software: Trust and Interface Design. A single-author review following PRISMA 2020. I screened 124 records down to 17 studies, sorted the findings into trust factors that depend on the task and design cues that depend on the interface, and rated each study's evidence on a five-part rubric.
